# Website audit and actionable redesign plan

Prepared 29 September 2026 for [Praneeth’s CS Field Notes](https://notes.praneeth-suresh-s.workers.dev/). Repository revision inspected: `35a2fff`. This is an audit and a proposed implementation plan. No published copy, templates, tests, or deployment were changed.

The named `Website-Audit-Codex-Handoff.md` was not present in this checkout or its nearby workspace. The user supplied its prior audit text directly; that material was treated as historical context and every finding used below was rechecked against the current repository, generated output, and rendered site. This document replaces `Website-Audit-and-Redesign-Plan-Preliminary.md`.

## Decision brief

The primary readers are recruiters and potential collaborators. The main visitor outcome is knowledge exploration. Unfinished notes should stay public and visibly identify their state. The site should use a quiet, direct, Notion-inspired reading style and the coherent interaction mechanics demonstrated by [Colin Moy’s site](https://colin-moy.webflow.io/). Those choices supersede older Stripe-inspired visual decisions in `.beryl/agent/design-tree.md` for this proposal; the design tree should be updated only after this plan is ratified.

The redesign should let a first-time visitor answer three questions quickly: who Praneeth is, what he actually built or investigated, and where to start reading. The archive should then make a deep visit easy without implying that an open notebook is a reviewed reference work. The immediate repair priority is factual trust and broken assets, followed by publication status, search and navigation, and then visual simplification.

## Editorial recommendations and unverified claims

These are judgments or publication questions, not observed broken behavior:

- Home and About should lead with a plain identity, current question, and named artifact. Phrases such as “rigorous,” “inspectable,” and “systems with evidence” should be followed by a specific example, not repeated as self-description. This is a subjective clarity recommendation supported by the short gateway copy and CV contrast.
- All four project pages use the same constraint/construction/published-evidence structure. Keep common metadata, but give each a distinct case study: your role, one hard decision, a real artifact/screen/output, a limitation, and its present state. Beryl’s “guarantee layer,” Notes’ “reliable” and “fast,” and SIAP’s “publishable” wording need bounded evidence or narrower language.
- Research Taste currently lists 13 broad themes. Select two or three current questions with an explanation of what evidence would change your view; retain the wider bibliography as an archive.
- Start Here is a switchboard and labels subscribing “Best.” Offer a recruiter/collaborator path and two knowledge paths with real page sequences; subscription is optional follow-up.
- The public labels Blog, Writing, Research, research trail, and Research Taste overlap. Use Notes, Projects, Writing, and About as primary destinations, with Search visible; make Contact, CV, RSS, and Errata utility links. Keep existing URLs and redirect only after link-map approval.
- The newsletter request is currently a `mailto:` path, while RSS is the working subscription. Do not promise a maintained monthly cadence or cancellation behavior until the owner confirms the process.
- Personal metrics, roles, project outcomes, manuscript state, claimed tests, and anecdotal incidents require owner records or direct artifacts before stronger copy is published. The audit did not independently confirm the CV’s 1,000+ documents/day, sub-200 ms retrieval, 20% OCR gain, ~40% review-time reduction, project participation, or future-dated research benchmarks. Treat these as **unverified**, not false.
- Research syntheses should identify paper findings, author interpretations, and open conjectures at claim level. The “Method/Results” sections in `a-research-program-for-inspectable-ml.md` read like an empirical study while the piece is chiefly a position/synthesis; label the method and evidence accordingly. Other early AI essays have few direct source links. Beryl and forecasting write-ups need sample outputs and linked underlying evidence where claims depend on it.
- Decorative blog/home imagery should be kept only when it communicates a subject-specific fact or useful atmosphere at reasonable size. The images were viewed in the rendered pages; originality/licensing and permission to reuse third-party diagrams were not established by this audit.

## Proposed information architecture and publication contract


| Destination | Single job                                                      | Content order                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home        | Establish identity and send the reader toward proof or learning | Two-sentence identity/current focus; two selected artifacts; three selected reading entries; topic map; contact.                                               |
| Notes       | Search and browse the open knowledge archive                    | Search; clearly scoped topic rows; curated paths; recent reviews; full topic list.                                                                             |
| Topic root  | Set scope and choose a useful first note                        | Short scope/prerequisites; recommended sequence; full child listing; sources. Keep a separate long essay only if the root genuinely teaches something.         |
| Nested note | Answer one bounded question honestly                            | Title; type/status/date; question; explanation/example; assumptions/limits; source trail; reasoned next link. Short notes need only the parts that earn space. |
| Projects    | Show inspectable contributions                                  | Selected work ordered by evidence and audience relevance; role/status; one concrete output per project; route to full dossier.                                 |
| Writing     | Present developed arguments and reported project work           | Selected essays; chronological archive; simple searchable topics.                                                                                              |
| About       | Give human/professional context                                 | Current role, interests, relevant experience, selected evidence, CV, contact.                                                                                  |
| Contact     | Make a conversation easy                                        | What you are open to; direct email/link choices; what context helps.                                                                                           |

Publication status is distinct from content type. **Working note** means incomplete or not yet checked; **Reviewed note** means Praneeth checked claims, examples, links, and layout on a recorded date (not peer review); **Archived** means retained but no longer maintained, with a reason and current pointer where possible. Existing notes default to Working note. Store status, `reviewedAt`, known gaps, and related-link reasons in a checked-in publication sidecar keyed by the stable Notion root page ID or child `blockId`, rather than editing normalized ingestion output that a pull can overwrite. `pages-build` joins the sidecar to page records; `site-styling` presents it; search/sitemap use the same data. Missing/duplicate IDs fail the build, and no automated process silently promotes a note to Reviewed. Known factual errors are corrected immediately even on Working notes.

## Design system proposal

The subject is a public technical notebook and portfolio. Its single visual signature should be a **reading margin**: a narrow, quiet rail showing the note’s status, source/review date, and one annotated “from / next” link. On a phone, the same facts become a compact metadata line above the article and a continuation after it. This makes provenance and exploration visible without a graph interface or decorative dashboard.


| Token/element       | Initial specification to prototype on real pages                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Palette             | Paper`#F7F7F5`, ink `#202124`, secondary text `#5F666A`, rule `#D9DDDC`, link/focus blue `#315F9C`, working-status amber `#825B21`. Check actual contrast on backgrounds and states.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Type                | System sans for body and restrained display; headings use weight/spacing rather than a separate decorative face, following the requested Notion-like directness. Self-hosted or system monospace only for code, keys, and metadata. Prototype body 17–18 px / 1.55–1.65, desktop H1 36–40 px, mobile H1 30–34 px; measure actual line length and math behavior before freezing tokens. |
| Width and rhythm    | Main reading measure about 66–76 characters; optional margin rail outside it. Wider figure/table region only when needed. Sentence case, clear section gaps, plain archive rows, no gratuitous numbered decoration.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Surface and imagery | Mostly paper and ink with one accent. Real project screens, worked diagrams, or no image. Compress and size source images; load below-the-fold images lazily with explicit dimensions.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Motion              | Navigation disclosure/feedback only where it clarifies state, about 120–180 ms, no essential animation; reduced-motion mode removes it. Avoid forced dark Home and per-section visual themes unless user testing shows they improve wayfinding.                                                                                                                                           |

The useful principle from Colin Moy is that a small vocabulary of destinations produces predictable in-place disclosure at desktop and mobile. For this site, use that principle sparingly: a compact, hash-addressable set of curated reading paths on Home or Notes may reveal a short selection, while primary pages remain ordinary links and deep links always work. The reference’s animated letterforms and graphic density do not serve long math/code reading. Prototype Home, Binary Search or a difficult math note, C++ Memory Ownership, and Beryl with actual content; compare desktop and mobile before changing shared templates.

Minimum editorial triage is **125 notes × 15 minutes ≈ 31 hours**, plus **19 posts × 30–60 minutes ≈ 10–19 hours** and **4 project dossiers × 2–4 hours ≈ 8–16 hours** to collect/verify artifacts. This 49–66 hour baseline is only scope/status/links/example review; deep technical fact-checking and rewriting may take substantially longer, especially the 4,000–5,300-word notes. The author must confirm personal metrics, roles, results, incident accounts, and project availability before they are featured. No other user-judgment question blocks the plan.

Implementation order is deliberate: first make truth and images safe; then establish status and route metadata; then write the editorial core and prototype real pages; finally apply the shared visual system and run complete route verification. Add deterministic tests only for behavior that can regress (IDs/status, route generation, link integrity, image persistence, search ranking, fidelity). Use the repository’s existing `./.beryl/scripts/check.sh` and `./scripts/check-project.sh`, then Playwright MCP for changed HTML. Do not weaken existing fidelity tests. After approval, record the new publication-metadata boundary and revised visual decisions in `.beryl/agent/design-tree.md` and, if the sidecar becomes a lasting architectural boundary, an ADR. They are not changed in this planning turn.
